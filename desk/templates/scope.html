How a report moves

new → triaging → (needs-info ⇄) → accepted → fixing → patched → disclosed → awarded → paid, or closed as duplicate, invalid, out of scope or withdrawn. Every move is in the log; every ticket says whether an internal ticket tracks its fix and which PR closed it.

What is published when. A report, its status and its thread are public from the start (unless it was filed private). The team's assessment of a confirmed, unfixed bug (its tier, verdict and internal ticket) is published when the fix ships: until then, a ranked list of what still works against the network is the one thing this desk should not be. Which fixes land before mainnet is the team's planning and is not published at all.
