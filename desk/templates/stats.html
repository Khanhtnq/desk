How this is counted

Reports filed, and the days and hours they arrive, count every report, including those read in private until a fix ships. The log's length is the log's own. Everything else, researchers included, counts public reports as the report list shows them: a tier appears once its fix has shipped, and an award once its ticket is marked awarded. The page is the same for everyone who reads it. Days and hours are UTC.
